Japan's elimination from the 2018 FIFA World Cup prompts the Japan Football Union to start a programme -- the Blue Lock Project -- to scout and train young players in preparation for the 2022 World Cup. Yoichi Isagi, the top striker on his high school team, receives an invitation to this programme soon after his team fails to make Nationals as a result of a critical error he made in the last play of a game -- passing to a less-skilled teammate since he lacked the ego to attempt the goal himself. Blue Lock's director is Jinpachi Ego, who intends to “destroy Japanese loser football” by introducing a radical new training regimen: isolate 300 young strikers in the prison-like institution called “Blue Lock" and put them through rigorous training aimed at creating “the world's greatest striker" by shaping one's ego to have the confidence to make game-winning goals. Characters: Yoichi Isagi, Maaguru batchira, Hyoma chigiri, Ransuke kunigami, rin itoshi, shoei Baro, seishiro Nagi, Reo Mikage, Jinpachi Ego, sae itoshi.
